## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-conviction quantitative execution, precision telemetry, and refined computational elegance. Built for institutional traders, high-frequency operators, and sophisticated retail asset managers, the interface departs from dull muddy dark themes into an ultra-crisp slate-indigo chromatic spectrum illuminated by vibrant data signals.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Minimalism** with **Luminous Glass Surfaces**:
- **Clarity over Clutter:** Zero visual noise, razor-sharp metric delineations, and deliberate density distributions where data remains immediately actionable without visual fatigue.
- **Deep Spectral Depth:** Midnight slate grounds layered beneath cool indigo-tinted cards, illuminated by directional cyan and ultraviolet optical glows.
- **Tactile High-Density Feedback:** Micro-borders with sub-pixel crispness, luminous interactive states, and authoritative institutional weight across data tables, depth ladders, and execution tickets.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined luminous dark architecture. It uses cool, chromatic slates instead of achromatic neutral grays to preserve vibrant contrast without causing optical eye strain.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0c0e17`):** Deep oceanic void; provides infinite baseline contrast.
- **Primary Surface (`#141724`):** Base card and container tier with cool slate-indigo pigment.
- **Elevated Surface (`#1a1e30`):** Floating modules, active tables, and overlay flyouts.
- **Border Tone (`#2a314b`):** Precision 1px structure preventing color bleed across dense charting panels.
- **Brand Accents:**
  - **Electric Indigo (`#6366f1`):** Primary actions, focal states, active tabs, and liquidity highlights.
  - **Luminous Cyan (`#06b6d4`):** Secondary data signals, volume delta lines, execution anchors, and downward trends.
  - **Ultraviolet (`#a855f7`):** Predictive curves, derivative layers, and premium tier indicators.

### Trading & Market Dynamics
- **Bullish Momentum:** Vivid Korean Market Red (`#ef4444`) with an ambient pulse (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.16)`) for upper breakouts and buy-side volume.
- **Bearish Drift / Downside Pricing:** Crisp Cool Cyan-Blue (`#3b82f6` / `#06b6d4`) with glow states (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.16)`) for sell-side order books and bid-ask spreads.
- **Neutral / Parity:** Cool Ice Gray (`#94a3b8`) for unchanged price tick metrics.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces immediate legibility through a deliberate triple-typeface system:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headers):** Structural, geometric, and modern. Delivers authoritative impact on major account valuations, module titles, and high-level navigational anchors.
2. **Geist (Body & Meta):** Highly engineered grotesque with neutral glyph geometry. Maintains crisp legibility in ultra-dense analytical layouts without visual clutter or awkward wrapping.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Quantitative Data):** Monospaced glyphs with tabular numbers (`tnum`) applied universally across price feeds, order books, tick sizes, order entry inputs, and timestamps to eliminate column jitter during streaming updates.

### Rules of Application
- Numerical metrics must never truncate or wrap; apply `white-space: nowrap` and tabular numeral OpenType settings (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1`).
- All metadata table labels and column headers must use uppercase tracking (`label-caps`) with 60% opacity (`#94a3b8`) for immediate hierarchical contrast against active values.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around high-density operational telemetry organized into a dynamic CSS Grid workspace that maximizes horizontal viewport availability.

### Grid Engine & Breakpoints
- **Trading Console Grid (Desktop > 1440px):** 12-column modular canvas with a permanent compact command rail (64px), flexible chart center (spans 6–8 columns), order book and depth ladder (spans 2–3 columns), and trade execution panel (spans 2–3 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8-column reflow. Order placement docks into an expandable slide-over bottom sheet or consolidated right-hand accordion.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked mode with persistent sticky market-ticker bar and fixed execution quick-action footer.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Inner card padding uses tight modular intervals (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to prioritize information density.
- Visual breathability is concentrated in analytical zones: charts must retain at least `space-lg` to `space-xl` equivalent padding buffers around price axes, preventing visual collision between candlestick wicks and panel perimeters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use murky drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **Luminous Stratification**: a combination of dark-spectrum tonal layers, precision hairline borders, and cyan/indigo optical glows.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#0c0e17`. Absolute backdrop.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Card & Module Surface):** Solid `#141724` encased with a 1px continuous border of `#2a314b`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Active Panels):** `#1a1e30` with a top-lit hairline border (`1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)` to `#2a314b` gradient). Casts a faint ambient indigo glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(12, 14, 23, 0.8), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Context Menus, and Drawers):** Translucent `#141724` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and edged with `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`. Emits an expansive deep slate aura: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`.

### Market State Illumination
Critical state changes use self-illuminating glass badges. Bullish surges trigger a soft red perimeter bloom (`0 0 12px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`), while downside price movements cast a cool cyan-blue luminescence (`0 0 12px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The shape system pairs modern software smoothness with sharp analytical precision. 

- **Containers & Chart Panels:** Base corner radius is `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`), maintaining structural discipline across multi-pane dashboard configurations.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Pill Filters:** Interactive components leverage either `0.5rem` or full pill enclosures (`9999px`) for contextual quick-select chips (e.g., time intervals `1m`, `5m`, `1H`, `1D`).
- **Input Fields & Form Selectors:** Fixed at `0.5rem` to match adjacent execution triggers, ensuring clean alignment along the vertical grid axis.

## Components

### Buttons & Order Triggers
- **Primary Execution (Buy / Long):** Background filled with Korean Market Red (`#ef4444`), contrasting pure white typography (`#ffffff`), with a subtle inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Hover elevates saturation with a localized red outer glow.
- **Secondary Execution (Sell / Short):** Background filled with Cyan-Blue (`#3b82f6` or `#06b6d4`), high-contrast dark-navy or white text, and an outer cyan halo on hover.
- **Utility & Standard Actions:** Surface `#1a1e30`, border `1px solid #2a314b`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover shifts border to Indigo `#6366f1` with background elevation.

### Data Tables & Order Books
- **Header:** Sticky positioning, background `#141724`, text `label-caps` in `#94a3b8`, separated by a sharp 1px `#2a314b` bottom divider.
- **Rows:** Height fixed at 28px for dense books and 40px for active positions. Monospaced numeric alignment (`JetBrains Mono`). Hover initiates an instantaneous background transition to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`.
- **Depth Visualization Bars:** Horizontal volume bars render in the row background with an ultra-soft opacity: Red (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`) and Cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`).

### Input Fields & Numeric Spinners
- **Default State:** Container `#0c0e17`, border `1px solid #2a314b`, rounded `0.5rem`, text `label-numeric-md` in `#f8fafc`. Placeholder text `#475569`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to Cyan `#06b6d4` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`).
- **Steppers:** Integrated right-aligned micro-triggers for leverage and tick increments, separated by vertical internal hairline dividers.

### Chips, Badges & Tickers
- **Market State Badges:** Pill-shaped, 4px vertical by 8px horizontal padding. Background combines 10% fill of token color (Red or Cyan) with matching solid text and an ultra-fine border (`1px solid rgba(currentColor, 0.3)`).
- **Timeframe Selector Chips:** Segmented pill array on `#0c0e17`. Inactive chips render with `#94a3b8` text without borders; active chip snaps to `#1a1e30` with a clean `#6366f1` border and luminous text.

### Cards & Modular Windows
- Built from base `#141724` with structural `#2a314b` outer strokes. Module headers include dedicated drag handles, asset pair icons, full-screen expansion toggles, and compact status dots indicating live WebSocket latency.
